Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy covers the "Sage & Sweater Pin Assistant", an internal tool operated by Sage & Sweater (sageandsweater.etsy.com) that connects to Pinterest through the Pinterest API.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by the owner of Sage & Sweater to manage the shop's own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other Pinterest users.

What data it accesses

  • The Sage & Sweater Pinterest account's own boards and Pins, to create, schedule and organize Pins that link to our own product listings.
  • Basic analytics for our own Pins (such as impressions and clicks), to see which designs people are interested in.

The tool does not access other Pinterest users' boards, Pins, or personal information.

How data is stored and protected

The Pinterest access token and analytics are stored on a private, access-restricted server controlled by the shop owner. They are not shared with anyone else.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent or share Pinterest data with third parties.
  • We do not use Pinterest content or API data to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • We do not use Pinterest data for advertising targeting outside Pinterest.

Retention and deletion

Analytics are kept only as long as they are useful for running the shop. Access can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account's settings, after which the stored token no longer works and is deleted.

Shoppers

Purchases happen on Etsy, and Etsy's privacy policy applies to orders and buyer information. This tool does not receive buyer information.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send us a message through our Etsy shop at sageandsweater.etsy.com.
